Our app

Faal

Coming soon to iPhone

Once a day, a parrot picks one card from twelve face-down cards, and the card holds a single couplet of Hafez. Read it in Persian with an English, Urdu or Hindi rendering, and take a moment with it. Faal is for reflection, not prediction.

الا یا ایها الساقی ادر کأسا و ناولها

که عشق آسان نمود اول ولی افتاد مشکل‌ها

Hafez, Ghazal 1 · Couplet 1

  • One faal a day, the same for everyone who opens the app.
  • English, Urdu and Hindi, with an optional Roman transliteration.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Privacy policy
