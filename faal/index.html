Faal

Coming soon to iPhone

Once a day, a parrot picks one card from twelve face-down cards, and the card holds a single couplet of Hafez. Read it in Persian with an English, Urdu or Hindi rendering, and take a moment with it. Faal is for reflection, not prediction.

The ritual

One faal a day

Hold a question in mind, and the parrot draws for you. Twelve cards are dealt face down and only one is turned over, so the couplet you get is the couplet you sit with until tomorrow. Everyone who opens the app that day receives their own draw from the same curated pool.

Reading it

In your own language

The Persian is always there, and beside it an English, Urdu or Hindi rendering — the Hindi adapted rather than transliterated. An optional Roman transliteration follows South Asian pronunciation, and turning the phone sideways shows the couplet large.
