Faal Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Last updated 6 November 2026

Faal does not collect any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us.

Who we are

Faal is made by Hashiya Studio LLC, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Faal app for iPhone and this website.

What stays on your phone

Faal keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you left it: your settings (language, skin, layout, transliteration, sound and vibration), today’s faal, and which purchases are unlocked. This information is stored only on your iPhone, is never sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party tools that collect data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • We never sell or share personal data, because we don’t have any.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as skins) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple gives developers sales reports without personal information. Apple’s handling of your purchase is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don’t add you to any mailing list, and we delete support emails once the matter is closed unless we need to keep them for legal reasons.

This website

This site uses no cookies or analytics. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security and operation.

Children

Faal is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If Faal ever begins to collect any data, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Contact
